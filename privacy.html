3. Our Privacy Commitment

We do not collect personal information from our users.

Our Apps are designed with privacy as a core principle. We have intentionally built our Apps to function without collecting, storing, processing, or transmitting any personal data. This means:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or contact information through our Apps
  • We do not collect location data or device identifiers
  • We do not track your browsing history or app usage patterns
  • We do not collect any biometric data or voice recordings
  • We do not create user profiles or accounts
  • We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies within our Apps
  • We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data

4. How Our Apps Work

Our Apps process all data locally on your device. Any content filtering, text conversion, or other functionality occurs entirely on your device without transmitting information to our servers or any third-party servers.

We do not have access to the content you view, filter, or create using our Apps.

5. Third-Party Services

Our Apps may be distributed through third-party app stores (such as Apple App Store or Google Play Store). These platforms may collect information about your device and app downloads according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these platforms.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Apps do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 16. Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal information from children that we could inadvertently collect or store.

7. Data Security

Because we do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal information that could be compromised. All App functionality occurs locally on your device, and we do not maintain databases or servers that store user information.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the GDPR, you have certain rights regarding your personal data. However, since we do not collect, store, or process any personal data, these rights are not applicable in the context of our Apps:

  • Right of Access: There is no personal data held by us to access
  • Right to Rectification: There is no personal data held by us to correct
  • Right to Erasure: There is no personal data held by us to delete
  • Right to Restrict Processing: We do not process any personal data
  • Right to Data Portability: There is no personal data held by us to transfer
  • Right to Object: We do not process any personal data

If you have any concerns about your privacy when using our Apps, please contact us at the email address provided below.
